Homemade Cookies in Fullerton, CA

Kassey's Kitchen bakes two kinds of homemade cookies to order in Fullerton, CA: brownie cookies and chocolate chip cookies. Both are $5 for 2, made from scratch in small batches, and ready for pickup when you order at least 3 to 5 days ahead.

Brownie Cookies, $5 for 2

Rich, fudgy brownie-meets-cookie with crisp edges and gooey centers, packed with chocolate.

Chocolate Chip Cookies, $5 for 2

Thick, chewy, loaded with semi-sweet chocolate chips and a hint of vanilla. A classic done right.

How to order cookies

Send an order request with how many cookies you need and the date you need them. Kassey replies within 24 to 48 hours to confirm details, pricing and your pickup date. Pay by Venmo, Zelle, Apple Cash, Cash App, PayPal or cash.

Cookies for parties and events

For events, cookies also come as party favor bags: 2 cookies per kraft bag with a ribbon and a custom tag, $5.50 per bag, minimum 20 bags. See cookie party favor bags and dessert catering.
