How to order mini cake loaves

Send an order request with how many you need, your date and the flavor you are hoping for. Kassey replies within 24 to 48 hours to confirm flavors, pricing and your pickup date.

  • Pickup details are confirmed with your order. The exact address is shared when your order is confirmed.
  • Payment by Venmo, Zelle, Apple Cash, Cash App, PayPal or cash. A deposit may be required for custom or catering orders.
  • Everything is made in a home kitchen that handles nuts, dairy, wheat and eggs, so allergen-free products cannot be guaranteed. Mention any allergies in your order request.
