Banana Bread in Fullerton, CA

Kassey's Kitchen bakes banana bread from scratch to order in Fullerton, CA, in two sizes: a mini loaf for $5 and a full-size loaf for $25. Order at least 3 to 5 days ahead and pick it up fresh.

Super moist banana bread in an adorable mini loaf, perfect for a sweet breakfast or afternoon snack.

Large Banana Bread Loaf, $25

Our fan-favorite banana bread in a full-size loaf: incredibly moist, perfectly sweet, great for sharing.

From the oven

Every loaf is mixed, filled and baked to order in small batches.

How to order banana bread

Send an order request with the size, how many and your date. Kassey replies within 24 to 48 hours to confirm details, pricing and your pickup date.

  • Pickup details are confirmed with your order. The exact address is shared when your order is confirmed.
  • Payment by Venmo, Zelle, Apple Cash, Cash App, PayPal or cash. A deposit may be required for custom or catering orders.
  • Everything is made in a home kitchen that handles nuts, dairy, wheat and eggs, so allergen-free products cannot be guaranteed. Mention any allergies in your order request.
