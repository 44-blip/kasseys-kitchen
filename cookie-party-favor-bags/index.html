Cookie Party Favor Bags in Fullerton, CA

Kassey's Kitchen makes cookie party favor bags in Fullerton, CA: 2 fresh-baked cookies per bag for $5.50 each, with a minimum of 20 bags. Book at least 2 weeks before your event.

What's in each bag

  • 2 cookies per bag
  • Kraft bag with ribbon
  • Custom tag with your message
  • Minimum 20 bags

Favors for any event

Individually packaged fresh-baked treats for guests to take home, for birthday parties, baby showers, bridal showers, quinceañeras, corporate events, graduation parties, weddings and holiday parties.

How to order favor bags

Send an order request with your event date, the number of bags (20 or more) and the message for your tags. Kassey replies within 48 hours to confirm details and pricing. A deposit secures your date, and full payment is due 3 days before your event.

  • Pickup details are confirmed with your order. The exact address is shared when your order is confirmed.
  • Individual orders are pickup only. Delivery is available for catering and other large orders for an additional fee.
  • Everything is made in a home kitchen that handles nuts, dairy, wheat and eggs, so allergen-free products cannot be guaranteed. Mention any allergies in your order request.
