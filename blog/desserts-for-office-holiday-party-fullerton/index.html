Individual Items for a Simple Setup

An office party rarely has a full kitchen. You may have a break room with a counter, a conference table, or just a desk. Individual items remove the need for plates, forks, and serving spoons. Mini banana bread, chocolate conchas, and mini cake loaves are single baked items. People can grab one and walk away.

Brownie cookies and chocolate chip cookies are also individual by nature. Two cookies for $5 means you can order several sets and spread them on a platter. No slicing, no plating, no mess. The large banana bread loaf is the only item that requires a knife, but it is still simple to cut at the office.

Cookie party favor bags take this one step further. Each bag is pre-packed with two cookies and a custom tag. You can set a basket of bags near the door and let people take one on their way out. The minimum is 20 bags, so this works for a medium to large office. The custom tag can say something like "Happy Holidays from the team" or a specific message you choose.

Dietary Needs for a Crowd

Kassey's Kitchen cannot guarantee items that are free of a specific allergen. Everything is made in a home kitchen that handles nuts, dairy, wheat, and eggs, so cross-contact is possible. If someone in your office has a severe allergy, mention it in your order request. Kassey cannot promise a safe product for someone with a serious allergy.

For a crowd, the practical approach is to ask your coworkers about allergies before you order. If someone has a nut allergy, for example, you may want to avoid items that could contain nuts. But do not assume any item is without nuts. The home kitchen handles nuts, so even a plain chocolate chip cookie could have trace amounts. Be clear with Kassey about any concerns, and she will respond within 24 to 48 hours.
