How catering booking works

  1. Contact Kassey

    Send a request with your event date, guest count and what you have in mind.

  2. Chat with Kassey

    She reaches out within 48 hours to go over options, customize your menu and quote.

  3. Lock it in

    A deposit secures your date. Full payment is due 3 days before your event.

  4. Delivered fresh

    Everything arrives fresh and styled on the day of your event. You just enjoy it.

Lead time, pickup and delivery

Please book at least 2 weeks before your event. Weekends fill up fast, so book early. Pickup is free, and delivery is available for catering orders for an additional fee.

Catering questions

How far ahead should I book dessert catering?

At least 2 weeks before your event. Weekends fill up fast, so book early.

Do you deliver catering?

Yes. Delivery is available for catering orders for an additional fee. Pickup is free.

How does payment work for catering?

A deposit secures your date and full payment is due 3 days before your event. Kassey accepts Venmo, Zelle, Apple Cash, Cash App, PayPal and cash.

Can the desserts match my theme?

Yes. All packages are fully customizable, and the sweet treat table is styled to your theme.

  • Everything is made in a home kitchen that handles nuts, dairy, wheat and eggs, so allergen-free products cannot be guaranteed. Mention any allergies in your order request.
